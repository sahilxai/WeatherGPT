================================================================================
NATIONAL DISASTER MANAGEMENT & EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS STANDARD OPERATING MANUAL
================================================================================

1. FLASH FLOODS & SEVERE WATERLOGGING PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Immediate Response Actions:
- Do NOT walk, swim, or drive through flood waters. "Turn Around, Don't Drown". Just 6 inches (15 cm) of moving water can knock an adult down, and 12 inches (30 cm) can sweep away a car.
- Evacuate immediately if advised by local authorities or if living in low-lying, flood-prone zones.
- Move to higher ground or upper floors immediately. Avoid basements and enclosed ground-level areas.
- Disconnect electricity main breakers and gas valves before floodwaters enter your property.
- Never touch electrical equipment or power cords if wet or standing in water.
- Boil drinking water for at least 1 minute or use certified water purification tablets before consumption.
- Flood Emergency Contacts: National Disaster Helpline (112 / 911), Coast Guard Search & Rescue.

2. CYCLONES, HURRICANES & SEVERE TYPHOONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Preparation Before Landfall (24-48 Hours):
- Board up glass windows with marine plywood or apply heavy-duty hurricane storm shutters.
- Secure loose outdoor furniture, antennas, solar panels, and garbage bins.
- Stock emergency supply kit for a minimum of 72 hours:
  * Non-perishable food (canned goods, energy bars)
  * Clean drinking water (4 liters per person per day)
  * Battery-powered or hand-crank emergency radio
  * High-lumen LED flashlights and spare batteries
  * First-aid kit with essential prescription medicines
  * Water-sealed pouch with government IDs, insurance papers, and cash.
During the Storm:
- Stay indoors in an interior, windowless room, hallway, or closet on the lowest level that is not prone to flooding.
- Beware the "Eye of the Storm": A temporary calm does NOT mean the storm is over. Violent winds will resume suddenly from the opposite direction.

3. EARTHQUAKE RESPONSE PROTOCOL ("DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inside a Building:
- DROP onto your hands and knees immediately to prevent being thrown down.
- COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table, desk, or interior architectural beam.
- HOLD ON to your shelter until shaking stops.
- Do NOT run outside during active shaking; falling masonry, glass, and facade tiles pose the highest casualty risk.
- Do NOT use elevators under any circumstance.
Outdoors:
- Move away from buildings, streetlights, overhead powerlines, billboards, and brick chimneys into an open area.

4. EXTREME HEATWAVES & DROUGHT EMERGENCIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Critical Precautions:
- Drink water consistently before feeling thirsty. Avoid alcohol, high-caffeine beverages, and sugary carbonated drinks.
- Never leave infants, elderly individuals, or pets unattended in parked vehicles even for a single minute. Interior car temperatures can exceed 55°C (130°F) in 10 minutes.
- Recognize Heatstroke Symptoms: High body temperature (>40°C / 104°F), rapid pulse, hot and dry skin (absence of sweating), confusion, dizziness, or loss of consciousness. Call emergency services immediately and cool the victim with cold damp towels.
- Limit strenuous physical exertion to early mornings before 8:00 AM or evenings after 7:00 PM.

5. EMERGENCY CONTACT DIRECTORY & COMMUNICATION MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Unified Emergency Services: 112 (EU/India), 911 (USA/Canada), 999 (UK)
- Red Cross Disaster Relief & Reunification Hotline: 1-800-RED-CROSS
- Chemical / Gas Leak Hazmat Response: Fire & Rescue Hazmat Team
- Poison Control Center Hotline: Available 24/7
